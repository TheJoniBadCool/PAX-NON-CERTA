    Odds use raw strength raised to 1.35: counted stack units, +20% per unit above the strongest opponent, King +10%, Queen/Bishop +5%, XP/armour +5% per level, then stationary Tower defence ×3. XP/armour also reduce casualties by 5% per level; winners receive 50% casualty protection. Defeated lone Queens/Kings use 60%/75% base casualty risk before battle modifiers. Defeated casualties are captured: the winner chooses Prisoner or Execute. Prisoners never fight or route. Each retreat adds Routed: -20% strength and +25 percentage points casualty risk, up to 3 stacks. Strength penalties are averaged across a stack before the odds curve. Routed lasts 6 playable rounds after return; another retreat refreshes the timer.
